# Changelog

## 1.0.5
- Added Packet Fixer to fix "Packet too big" disconnects on join (the recipe sync packet went over the vanilla 8 MB limit)

## 1.0.4
- Removed Xaero's Minimap & World Map as that is handled by FTB Chunks now

## 1.0.3
- Added FTB Ultimine, FTB Essentials, FTB Chunks, and FTB XMod Compat
- Removed Alex's Mobs, Citadel, and Kilt
- Removed the KubeJS `/spawn` command; FTB Essentials now provides `/spawn`

## 1.0.2
- Added Immersive Paintings mod

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Minecraft 1.20.1, Fabric Loader 0.20.1, 129 mods (see MODLIST.md).
- New biomes (Oh The Biomes We've Gone), YUNG's structure suite, overhauled villages and new structures.
- Alex's Mobs (via Kilt), Naturalist, Friends & Foes and Creeper Overhaul.
- The full Macaw's suite, MrCrayfish's Furniture: Refurbished, Cluttered, Handcrafted, Supplementaries and more.
- Farmer's Delight plus the Let's Do farming and food mods.
- Mundus Foci content: Hearthstone blocks, Hearth Lamp, Lodestone Charm, Hearth Stew, Trail Mix,
  quality-of-life recipes and the `/mundus`, `/spawn`, `/coords` and `/playtime` commands.
